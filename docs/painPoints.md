# Pain Points

## BHA Entry

- BHA entry is often slow and repetitive.
- Existing software can require too many clicks to build a BHA.
- Engineers often need Excel alongside the software.
- Component libraries can be difficult to maintain.
- Comparing BHA options is often awkward.

## Jar Placement

- Neutral point can be difficult to determine quickly, especially in deviated wells.
- It is not always obvious whether sufficient weight can be transferred to the jar.
- Buckling conditions are not always presented clearly.
- Existing jar placement outputs can be difficult to interpret.
- Reports are often poorly formatted or require manual cleanup in Excel.

## Reporting

- Outputs should be easy to compare.
- Reports should show the engineering conclusion, not just raw calculations.
- Users should not need to rebuild reports manually in Excel.

## General UX

- Avoid duplicate data entry.
- Calculations should update live when inputs change.
- Important engineering results should be visible without navigating through menus.
- BHA editing should feel more like working in a spreadsheet.
