# WellBench Vision

**Version:** 0.2  
**Date:** 11 Aug 2026  
**Author:** Rob Hamilton

---

# Mission

To build the most intuitive and practical engineering workspace for drilling tools and Bottom Hole Assembly (BHA) design.

WellBench combines proven engineering calculations, modern visualisation and professional reporting to help engineers, supervisors and managers make better decisions faster.

The objective is not to replace large enterprise drilling software, but to provide a modern, affordable engineering platform for service companies and engineering teams.

---

# Target Market

WellBench is primarily aimed at:

- Independent drilling tool companies
- Fishing companies
- Jar rental companies
- Directional drilling companies
- Tool manufacturers
- Engineering consultants
- Regional service companies

Typical examples include companies similar to:

- Smith Gulf
- OSC
- Dynomax
- Abaco
- Vertex
- Gordon Technologies
- Hunting Cyclone Drilling
- Al Rashed
- SIGs Kuwait
- NAPESCO

These organisations often rely on spreadsheets, manual calculations and internally developed tools rather than expensive enterprise engineering software.

---

# Target Users

WellBench is designed for engineers and operational personnel including:

- Applications Engineers
- Drilling Engineers
- Field Engineers
- Tool Specialists
- Operations Managers
- Engineering Managers
- Workshop Supervisors
- Technical Sales Engineers

The software should remain approachable for occasional users while providing sufficient engineering depth for experienced specialists.

---

# Product Philosophy

WellBench should be:

- Engineering driven
- Simple to learn
- Fast to use
- Highly visual
- Vendor neutral
- Modular
- Fully traceable
- Professional enough for customer-facing reports

Users should spend their time solving engineering problems rather than learning the software.

---

# Design Principles

The interface should feel closer to modern productivity software than traditional engineering software.

Objectives include:

- Clean, uncluttered interface
- Consistent user experience
- Interactive visualisations
- Responsive layouts
- Desktop-first design
- Tablet-compatible architecture
- Focused mobile functionality for field use

Every engineering module should answer a practical operational question.

---

# Core Platform

The WellBench platform will provide:

- Project management
- BHA Builder
- Component Library
- Engineering database
- 3D visualisation
- Engineering reports
- Unit management
- Shared engineering model

Every engineering module will operate from the same underlying engineering model, ensuring consistency across the application.

---

# Initial Engineering Modules

## BHA Builder

Rapid construction and management of Bottom Hole Assemblies using an integrated component library.

---

## Jar Placement

The initial flagship engineering module.

Capabilities may include:

- Neutral point calculation
- Buoyancy
- Axial loads
- Jar placement optimisation
- Overpull analysis
- Compression and tension visualisation
- Engineering justification
- Professional reports

The emphasis is on helping engineers understand **why** a recommendation has been made, not simply presenting a calculated answer.

---

## Mechanical Analysis

Shared engineering calculations including:

- Centre of Gravity
- Axial Loads
- Neutral Point
- Stretch
- Buckling
- Torque & Drag
- Hydraulics

These calculations provide the foundation for higher-level engineering modules.

---

## 3D Engineering Viewer

Interactive visualisation of the BHA including:

- Component identification
- Compression and tension indication
- Neutral point visualisation
- Future engineering overlays

# Future Engineering Modules

## Fishing

Potential capabilities include:

- Fishing string design
- Overshot selection
- Spear selection
- Maximum safe overpull
- String stretch
- Jarring calculations
- Fishing reports

---

## Whipstock Planning

Potential capabilities include:

- Window geometry prediction
- Window visualisation
- BHA passability analysis
- Clearance analysis
- Milling behaviour modelling
- Window comparison studies

The long-term objective is to provide a digital representation of the window and evaluate BHA behaviour during milling and subsequent drilling operations.

---

## Additional Modules

Future development may also include:

- Tool selection assistance
- BHA comparison
- Engineering report generation
- Company-specific tool libraries
- Knowledge base and engineering guidance

### Motor Engineering (Under Evaluation)

There is potential to develop a dedicated motor engineering module covering motor selection, operating behaviour and performance prediction.

The commercial demand, user requirements and engineering scope will be validated through discussions with customers, directional drilling specialists and motor manufacturers before being included in the development roadmap.

The objective would be to provide practical engineering decision support rather than simply replicate existing motor performance software.

---

# Success Criteria

An engineer should be able to:

- Build a complete BHA in minutes.
- Understand the mechanical behaviour of the string.
- Optimise jar placement with confidence.
- Compare alternative BHAs.
- Validate engineering decisions visually.
- Produce professional customer-ready reports.
- Reuse engineering data across multiple workflows.

A manager should immediately recognise that WellBench improves engineering consistency, reduces manual effort and captures organisational knowledge.

---

# Version 1 Scope

Version 1 will focus on delivering a polished, production-quality engineering workspace centred on:

- BHA Builder
- Jar Placement
- Core mechanical calculations
- 3D engineering visualisation
- Professional reporting

Quality, usability and engineering accuracy take priority over the number of features.

---

# Out of Scope (Version 1)

The following are not planned for the initial release:

- Real-time rig monitoring
- WITSML integration
- Rig state detection
- Cost tracking
- Well planning
- Full drilling simulator
- Enterprise asset management
- Cloud collaboration

These may be considered in future releases if they support the overall product vision.
